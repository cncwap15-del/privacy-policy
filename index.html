Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CNC-WAP handles information in connection with its websites and software products, including ZOBaku, mobCAM, wapCAM, Kerago, and other applications published by CNC-WAP.

The type of information processed may vary depending on the particular website, application, or service being used. Not all CNC-WAP products collect or process all of the information described below.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the product or service being used, CNC-WAP may process the following information:

  • Username, where user accounts are supported
  • Email address, if provided voluntarily for support, licensing, or communication
  • IP address, browser type, operating system, and device information when using our websites
  • Application usage statistics or diagnostic information where such functionality is used
  • Cookies or local storage data used for language, session, or website preferences

CNC-WAP does not intentionally collect personal information that is not necessary for providing its products, services, licensing, or technical support.

2. How We Use Information

Information collected or voluntarily provided may be used for the following purposes:

  • To operate, maintain, and improve our applications, software, and websites
  • To provide technical support and customer service
  • To manage software licensing where applicable
  • To communicate important product or service information
  • To improve user experience
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations

3. Cookies and Local Storage

CNC-WAP websites may use cookies or local storage to remember language preferences, website settings, session information, and user experience preferences.

Users may disable or remove cookies through their web browser settings. Some website functionality may be affected when cookies or local storage are disabled.

4. Third-Party Services

Some CNC-WAP websites or applications may use or provide links to third-party services such as Microsoft Store, Google Play Services, Google Analytics, PayPal, YouTube, crash reporting services, or other external service providers.

These third-party services operate according to their own privacy policies and terms of use. CNC-WAP is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services.

5. Kerago - Local Image and Video Processing

Kerago is an image and video processing application for Windows that uses local AI-based processing to create visual transformations and effects.

Images and videos selected by the user for processing in Kerago are processed locally on the user's device.

Images and videos processed with Kerago are not uploaded to CNC-WAP servers and are not collected, stored, or transmitted by CNC-WAP.

AI-based image and video processing in Kerago is performed locally on the user's computer using AI models and processing components included with the application.

Kerago does not require users to create a CNC-WAP account and does not collect personal information through the application.

Files created or exported by Kerago are stored in locations selected or used by the user on their own device.

6. Software Licensing and Purchases

Some CNC-WAP products may require a software license or may be distributed through third-party stores such as Microsoft Store or Google Play.

Payments and purchases made through third-party stores or payment providers are processed by those providers according to their own privacy policies and terms. CNC-WAP does not receive or store users' credit card numbers, bank account credentials, or other payment credentials handled by those services.

7. Data Security

CNC-WAP takes reasonable measures to protect information provided by users from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

CNC-WAP does not knowingly collect personal information from children through Kerago.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

CNC-WAP may update this Privacy Policy when necessary to reflect changes to its products, services, or legal requirements.

Any updated version of this Privacy Policy will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
